# Art credits

## Farm animals

`assets/lpc/*.png` (chicken, cow, pig, sheep, llama):

**"LPC style farm animals" by Daniel Eddeland**
<https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-style-farm-animals>
License: [CC-BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) (also available under GPL 2.0).

Changes made by Farmies: the cow, pig, sheep and llama are scaled down to half size; poses such as
sleeping (closed eyes), held up (kicking legs) and, for the chicken, flapping and gliding wings
are derived from the walk animations at runtime (`src/animals.js`).
